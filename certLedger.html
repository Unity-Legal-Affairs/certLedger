certLedger Evidence Timeline

Local-first tracker for certified mail, screenshots, letters, evidence files, and dispute timeline work. No USPS scraping. No iframe trapdoor.

Operating rule

USPS remains a human lookup source. This page opens live USPS tracking pages and stores your manually captured status notes. Durable evidence is still your local screenshots, PDFs, letters, receipts, and exported JSON.

Run this from a local web server, not raw file://, if you want it to load evidence-index.json: python3 -m http.server 8000
